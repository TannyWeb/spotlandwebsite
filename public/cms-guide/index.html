📰 Adding a News Post

1 Click "Post" in the left menu
2 Click the "+ Create" button

You'll find this near the top of the page.

3 Fill in the form

Here's what each field means:

  • Title - The headline for your post (keep it short and clear)
  • URL Slug - Click the "Generate" button and it will create this automatically from your title
  • Published Date - When you want the post dated (today's date is already filled in)
  • Main Image - Click the grey box to upload a photo from your computer
  • Body - Type your news article here. It works like Word - you can make text bold, add bullet points, etc.
  • Featured (Pin to Homepage) - Tick this box if you want this post shown at the top of the "Latest from the Hub" section
  • YouTube Video URL - If you have a YouTube video, paste the link here (optional)
4 Click "Publish" when you're ready

You'll find this button at the bottom of the screen.

Remember

Your changes won't appear on the website until you click Publish!

🤝 Adding a New Service

1 Click "Service" in the left menu
2 Click "+ Create"

Same as when adding a post.

3 Fill in the form

Service fields explained:

  • Service Title - e.g., "Benefits Advice Drop-In"
  • URL Slug - Click "Generate"
  • Service Category - Choose the type:
    • Advice & Support
    • Health & Wellbeing
    • Youth Programs
    • Education & Learning
  • Main Image - Upload a welcoming photo
  • Service Description - Explain what this service offers
  • Schedule - When it runs (e.g., "Tuesdays 10am-2pm")
  • Schedule (Time-based) - You can also add specific time slots by clicking "Add item"
  • Get in Touch - Add phone number and email
  • Good to Know - Helpful info for nervous first-timers (e.g., "No need to book, just turn up!")
  • Feature on Homepage - Tick to show on homepage
4 Click "Publish"

Your new service will now appear on the website!

👥 Adding a Team Member

1 Click "Team Member" in the left menu
2 Click "+ Create"
3 Fill in the form
  • Name - Their full name
  • Role - Their job title (e.g., "Community Manager")
  • Photo - Upload their picture
  • Bio - A short description (2-3 sentences about them)
  • Display Order - Use numbers to control the order they appear (1 = first, 2 = second, etc.)
Display Order Tip

Want someone to appear first? Give them Display Order: 1. Second person gets 2, and so on.

📸 Creating a Photo Album

1 Click "Photo Gallery" in the left menu
2 Click "+ Create"
3 Fill in the form
  • Album Title - e.g., "Summer Fair 2024"
  • URL Slug - Click "Generate"
  • Description - A brief summary of the album
  • Cover Image - The main image shown on the gallery page (this is required)
4 Add your photos

Click "Add item" under the Photos section.

For each photo you add:

  • Upload the image - Choose your photo file
  • Add a Caption - This appears below the photo on the website
  • Add Alt Text - Describe what's in the photo (e.g., "Children playing football in the community garden"). This helps people who can't see images understand what's there.
Why Alt Text Matters

Alt text helps people using screen readers understand your images. It's also good for search engines!

5 Click "Publish"

🖼️ Uploading Images - Tips for Best Results

File Types

JPG or PNG work best for photos. Make sure your images are clear and not blurry!

Image Size

Images should be at least 800 pixels wide for good quality. Larger is usually better - the website will resize them automatically.

What's a Hotspot?

After uploading an image, you can click it and drag a circle to set a "hotspot". This tells the website which part of the image is most important, so it won't get cropped awkwardly on different screen sizes.

Example: If you upload a photo of someone's face, drag the hotspot circle to their face. That way, even if the image gets cropped on a phone screen, the face will still be visible.

❓ Common Questions (FAQ)

I made changes but can't see them on the website?
  • Make sure you clicked "Publish" (not just saved a draft)
  • Wait a minute or two, then refresh the page
  • Try opening the website in a "private" or "incognito" window
  • Do a hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R (or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac)
How do I delete something?
  • Open the item you want to delete
  • Click the three dots menu (⋮) at the top right
  • Select "Delete"
  • Confirm when asked
I made a mistake - can I undo?

Yes! The system keeps a history of your changes.

  • Click the clock icon at the top of the editing screen
  • You'll see previous versions
  • You can restore an older version if needed
SCREENSHOT: Clock/history icon location
The text looks funny - how do I format it?
  • When typing in body text, highlight the text you want to format
  • A small menu appears with options for bold, italic, bullet points, etc.
  • Click the formatting you want
How do I add a link in my text?
  • Highlight the words you want to make into a link
  • Click the link icon in the formatting menu
  • Paste the web address
  • Click "Apply" or "OK"
How do I log out?
  • Click your profile picture or initials in the top right corner
  • Click "Sign out"
